Sending the guidebook to a renter

Open your rental, tap Send to renter & QR sign, then Send to renter. Pick Messages or Mail and choose your guest. It opens in their phone's browser; they don't need the app.

Making the fridge sign

Put the guidebook file on any website you control, paste its web address into the QR section, then tap Make the fridge sign and print it.

Pro and restoring a purchase

The free version covers one rental. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited rentals and removes the Dock & Key line from your guidebooks. New phone? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.

Moving to a new phone

Your guidebooks travel in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Restore the backup and they come with it.
