Dock & Key

A guest guidebook your renters actually read.

Dock & Key is an iPhone app for vacation-rental hosts. Build your house guide on your phone — Wi-Fi, arrival, house how-tos, dock and lake steps, local picks, checkout — then text it straight to your renter or print a QR sign for the fridge. Guests just tap or scan. Nothing for them to download.
