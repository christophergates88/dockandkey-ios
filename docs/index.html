How it works

Write it once. Send it to every guest.

No website to set up, no template to wrestle with. If you can fill in a contact card, you can build a guidebook.

  1. Fill it in

    Open the app and add the rental's name, arrival details, Wi-Fi and house rules. Short fields, plain words.

  2. Add the how-tos

    The hot tub, the thermostat, the boat lift, where the life jackets live. Plus your favorite places to eat.

  3. Preview as a guest

    See exactly what your renter will see on their phone before anyone else does.

  4. Send to renter

    Tap Send to renter and pick Messages or Mail. With Pro, print a QR sign for the fridge too.

Questions

Things hosts ask.

Do my guests need to download an app?

No. The guidebook opens in their phone's browser from your text, your email, or the QR sign on the fridge.

Do I need a website?

No. Send to renter works with nothing but your phone. If you do have a website, the app can give you the guidebook as one web file to put there, and the QR sign can point to it.

What if I change the Wi-Fi password or add a hot tub?

Edit it in the app and send the new version. It takes a minute.

Will Ask the house make things up?

No. It only answers from what you wrote in the guidebook. If the answer isn't there, it tells the guest to message you.

Should I put the door code in it?

We suggest sending lock codes through your booking platform instead, since a guidebook can be forwarded.

Where is my information kept?

On your iPhone. Dock & Key has no account and no server, and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
