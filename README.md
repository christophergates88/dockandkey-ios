# Dock & Key — iOS app

Vacation-rental hosts build a guest guidebook on their phone: arrival & Wi-Fi,
house how-tos, dock & lake steps, places to eat, checkout and rules. They can
preview it as a guest, export it as a single self-contained web page (with a
built-in "Ask the house" helper that answers only from the guidebook), and
print a QR fridge sign. Guests just scan — nothing to download.

- Free tier: 1 rental.
- Pro (one-time purchase, StoreKit 2, product id
  `com.christophergates.dockandkey.pro`): unlimited rentals, no
  "Made with Dock & Key" line on exports. Create the product in
  App Store Connect with that exact id.
- Bundle id: `com.christophergates.dockandkey` · iOS 17+ · SwiftUI, no
  third-party dependencies, no server, no ongoing AI costs.

Open `DockAndKey.xcodeproj` in Xcode, pick your team under
Signing & Capabilities, choose your iPhone, press Run.
